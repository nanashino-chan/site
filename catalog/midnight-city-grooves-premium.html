Important distinction

Identifier, not proof

What an ISRC does—and does not—confirm

An ISRC identifies a particular sound recording. It does not, by itself, prove copyright ownership or grant permission to use the recording. Licensing is confirmed separately in writing for the specific project and scope.

  • ISRC = recording identification
  • License = permission for a defined use
  • Agreement or receipt = evidence of permission
