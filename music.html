Choosing Music for a Video or Brand?

Use the B2B music catalog to preview recordings and review track titles, ISRCs, UPCs, release dates and CSV track lists. Keep the title or ISRC of the recording you want to discuss.

Then visit the licensing page or send your project details, including where the music will appear and how it will be used. Listening previews do not themselves grant permission to publish the music in a new project.

制作向けの選曲・楽曲情報はB2Bカタログへ。利用したい曲が決まったら、曲名またはISRCと用途を添えてご相談ください。

Listening & Discovery FAQ

Where should I start listening?

Try one of the selected tracks or the mood player on this page. Open Discover when you want to browse album jackets, search for a track or explore more music.

How is this page different from Discover?

This page introduces the sound, listening moods and selected starting tracks. Discover lets you browse the music visually and search albums and tracks.

Where can I find music for study or quiet evenings?

Start with the Study and Focus guides for desk sessions, or the Relax and Quiet Nights guides for softer listening. Choose the sound and volume that feel comfortable to you.

Where can I find track details for a production?

The B2B catalog brings album previews, track titles, ISRCs, UPCs, release dates and CSV downloads together. Use it to prepare a track selection for your project.

How can I ask about using a track in a video?

Visit the licensing page or contact Nanashino-chan with the track title or ISRC and your project details. Confirm the applicable permission before publishing.
