Frequently Asked Questions

Are these examples licensed client projects?

They are public social media references. A direct client or licensing relationship is not claimed unless specifically confirmed.

How can I hear the music used in a post?

Open “View Album” beside the reference and select the named track. Each album page includes listening previews and recording information. Some album pages are still in preparation.

Can I use the same track in my project?

Review the licensing page and confirm permission for your specific project. A public post, artist credit or streaming availability does not by itself authorize a new use. Platform-library use depends on the applicable platform terms.

What should I send with a licensing inquiry?

Send the track title or ISRC, a short project description, publisher or client, platforms, territories, usage period, and whether the content includes sponsorship or paid advertising.
