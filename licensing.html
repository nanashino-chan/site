Useful answers

A few things to know before you buy.

Do I need a license for a personal social post?

For personal, non-commercial posts using Nanashino-chan tracks through an available Instagram, Facebook or TikTok music library, no additional license purchase from Nanashino-chan is required. Follow the platform’s rules. This does not cover standalone redistribution, external editing or commercial use.

Is this a subscription?

No subscription to Nanashino-chan is required. You purchase a license for its stated scope and term. A fixed-term license is not perpetual; extending the term or expanding the use may require an additional purchase.

Are business posts and paid ads the same plan?

No. For our licensing plans, business and brand use is commercial use. Ordinary non-ad online content and paid advertising have different plan scopes. CSR and nonprofit projects can be discussed individually.

How do I obtain the audio?

For in-app posts, use a music library that permits your intended use. For external editing, confirm an approved audio source before purchasing the license. Audio files are not included unless the product expressly says so. Streaming access or buying a recording does not by itself grant the required project license.

Can I start immediately after payment?

Use can begin after payment and completion of any track registration or approval required by the selected product. Check the start date, track coverage and project scope in the product terms. Custom projects follow the agreed written terms.

What happens if I receive a copyright claim?

Keep your receipt and License Certificate PDF. If a claim appears, contact us with the order details, track title or ISRC, video URL and claim screenshot. Support is available for licensed use within scope; a license does not guarantee that automated platform claims will never occur.

Does a license transfer ownership?

No. Copyright and master ownership remain with the rights holder. The license does not allow standalone resale, redistribution, claiming authorship or registering the recording in your own Content ID account.

What happens if music is used in advertising without the required license?

Without the necessary permission, platform rules or a rights-holder complaint may result in muted audio, removal of the video or interruption of distribution. Music being available when a video is published does not guarantee continued use.

Nanashino-chan does not tolerate unauthorized advertising use by advertisers, advertising agencies or production companies.

When unauthorized use is confirmed, we may request that the use stop and submit a copyright infringement notice to the relevant platform, as appropriate.

Before publishing an advertisement, confirm that your license covers the intended use. If you are unsure, contact us with the track title and a brief description of your advertisement.

Where can I follow Nanashino-chan’s latest activity?

Follow our official accounts for new music and videos, website updates and creative work.

  • YouTube, Instagram and TikTok: music videos and short-form videos.
  • LinkedIn: music and licensing initiatives, project updates and activity reports.
  • X and Bluesky: announcements and updates on our creative work.

With prior agreement, projects may be featured as music-use examples on our website, LinkedIn or other official channels.
